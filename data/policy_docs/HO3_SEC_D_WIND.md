# Section D - Wind and Hail

## SEC-1 [D.1] Windstorm and Hail
We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail. Loss to the interior of a
building is covered only if the wind or hail first creates an opening in a roof or wall.

## SEC-2 [D.2] Hurricane Deductible
Losses caused by a named hurricane are subject to a separate percentage deductible
of 2% of the dwelling coverage limit, not the flat all-peril deductible.
