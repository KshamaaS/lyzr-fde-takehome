# Section A - Water Damage

## SEC-1 [A.1] Sudden and Accidental Discharge
We cover direct physical loss caused by sudden and accidental discharge or overflow
of water or steam from within a plumbing pipe, heating, air conditioning, or automatic
fire protective sprinkler system, or from within a household appliance.

## SEC-2 [A.2] Freezing of Plumbing
We cover loss caused by freezing or bursting of a plumbing pipe or system, ONLY IF the insured has used
reasonable care to maintain heat in the building, or shut off the water supply and
drained the system. Loss caused by freezing where the building was vacant, unoccupied,
or under construction is EXCLUDED unless reasonable care was exercised.

## SEC-3 [A.3] Continuous Seepage - EXCLUDED
We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water over
a period of 14 days or more, whether known to the insured or not.

## SEC-4 [A.4] Flood - EXCLUDED
Loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of
water is EXCLUDED under this policy. Separate flood coverage is required.
