# Section E - General Conditions

## SEC-1 [E.1] Policy Period
We cover only losses occurring during the policy period. A policy reinstated after a
lapse provides no coverage for losses occurring during the lapse period.

## SEC-2 [E.2] Concealment or Fraud
We provide no coverage if an insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented any
material fact, engaged in fraudulent conduct, or made false statements relating to
this insurance, whether before or after a loss.

## SEC-3 [E.3] Duties After Loss
The insured must give prompt notice, protect the property from further damage, prepare
an inventory of damaged property, and cooperate in the investigation.

## SEC-4 [E.4] Deductible
The flat all-peril deductible of $1,000 applies to each covered loss, and is subtracted
from the amount of the covered loss before payment.
