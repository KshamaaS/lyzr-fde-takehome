# Claims Operations - Settlement Authority Matrix

## SEC-1 [O.1] Adjuster Authority Limits
Automated systems and Level 1 adjusters may settle claims up to $5,000 without
supervisory approval. Claims between $5,000 and $50,000 require a Senior Adjuster.
Claims above $50,000 require Claims Manager sign-off.

## SEC-2 [O.2] Mandatory Referral
Regardless of amount, the following must be referred to a human adjuster:
any claim with a fraud indicator, any claim where coverage is disputed or ambiguous,
any claim involving bodily injury, and any claim on a policy reinstated within
90 days of the loss date.

## SEC-3 [O.3] Reserve Setting
Initial reserve is set at the estimated loss amount less the applicable deductible.
Reserves above $25,000 require review within 5 business days.
