# Section C - Theft

## SEC-1 [C.1] Theft Coverage
We cover direct loss by theft, including attempted theft and loss of property from a
known location when it is likely the property has been stolen.

## SEC-2 [C.2] Reporting Requirement
The insured must notify the police promptly and provide a police report reference.
Failure to report to police may result in denial of the theft claim.

## SEC-3 [C.3] Notice Period
Notice of loss must be given to us promptly, and in no event more than 30 days after
the date of loss, unless the insured shows good cause for the delay.

## SEC-4 [C.4] Theft from Vacant Dwelling - EXCLUDED
Theft from a dwelling under construction, or a dwelling vacant for more than 60
consecutive days prior to the loss, is EXCLUDED.
