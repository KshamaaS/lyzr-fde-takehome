# Section B - Fire and Smoke

## SEC-1 [B.1] Fire and Lightning
We cover direct physical loss caused by fire or lightning. This includes damage from
smoke, soot, and firefighting activity including water damage from suppression efforts.

## SEC-2 [B.2] Smoke
We cover sudden and accidental damage from smoke. Loss caused by smoke from
agricultural smudging or industrial operations is EXCLUDED.

## SEC-3 [B.3] Intentional Loss - EXCLUDED
Loss arising from an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the
intent to cause a loss is EXCLUDED for all insureds.
